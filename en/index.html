02 / SERVICESCONSULTING ENGINEERING

Engineering and
infrastructure services.

We support project planning, design and delivery through consultancy and independent supervision services.

01

Consultancy

From pre-feasibility studies to detailed design and advice during construction.

  • Technical and financial pre-feasibility (Phase I) and feasibility (Phase II) studies and design.
  • Detailed engineering studies and final designs (Phase III).
  • Environmental, social and land studies.
  • Planning and design of urban and rural road projects.
  • Tender advisory services and project structuring.
  • Due diligence.
  • Risk and sustainability analysis.
  • Periodic reporting to oversight bodies and clients.
  • Technical advice during construction.
  • Railway studies and design.
  • Studies and design of complex structures.
  • Tunnel studies and design.
  • Airport studies and design.
  • Building studies and design.
  • Urban planning and landscape studies.
  • Design of waterfront promenades and similar structures.
02

Independent project supervision

Comprehensive monitoring of delivery, quality, resources and compliance with project obligations.

  • Technical, administrative, financial, legal and environmental supervision.
  • Schedule monitoring and execution control.
  • Verification of regulatory and contractual compliance.
  • Quality supervision of materials and construction processes.
  • Assessment of environmental and occupational health and safety compliance.
  • Support for project land management and social management.
  • Project budget and financial control.
